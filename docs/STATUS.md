## Answers to the owner's four questions

* **(2) Overlap**: `results/quality/train_test_overlap.md` (script `software/ai/dataset/check_overlap.py`). 3,642 training source images (old 192, DIV2K train 800,
  Flickr2K 2,650) compared with Set5, Set14, BSD100 and the 42 validation images: 0 exact pixel matches (also under flips/rotations), 0 whole-image near duplicates,
  0 region matches. The only exact matches are the deployed model's 12 held-out validation images, which are in val42 on purpose and in no training set. One
  candidate (BSD100/167062 vs flickr2k/000584, template score 0.974) was looked at: different images (a coyote in snow vs a train under a bridge), a
  flat-area false match, and the confirmation step now rejects it. Limit: crops that were also rotated or resized by an arbitrary factor are not searched.
  Note: the deployed model's checkpoint was selected on those 12 images (a small bias in its favour).
* **(3) Crops**: `results/quality/ladder_crops.md`, images `results/images/ladder_crops_*.png` (git-ignored; regenerate with `make_ladder_crops.py`). Picks by rule, not by hand:
  largest gain (BSD100/42049), median (Set5/baby), worst (BSD100/86068), largest R3q-over-R2q gain (Set5/butterfly). The new models remove the deployed model's
  edge ringing and are sharper on edges; on fine random texture (the worst case) none of them recovers detail, differences there are within 0.1 dB.
* **(4) R3q vs R2q**: yes, by more than the seed spread, but modestly. Mean gain +0.258 / +0.203 / +0.130 dB (val42 +0.238) against half-ranges of 0.04-0.15 dB;
  the two seeds of R3q are above both seeds of R2q on every set and on val42; per image (mean over seeds) R3q wins on 118 of 119 test images (mean +0.14 dB, worst -0.00). Only 2 seeds, so
  this is a consistent shift, not a significance test.
* **(d) Accumulators**: both candidates pass `acc_range.py` (`results/quality/acc_range_*.txt`): worst-case headroom 11-15 bits in the 32-bit accumulator, the 49-bit requantizer
  product fits (R3q L4 is the tightest: sign-aware worst case ±532k = 21 bits, observed ±73k). SHIFT becomes 22/22/21/22 (R2q) and 22/22/21/25 (R3q) instead of 24/23/21/24: a bitstream rebuild is needed for either.
