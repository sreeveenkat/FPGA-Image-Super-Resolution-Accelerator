# Cycle model: current design vs layer pipeline

Generated by `software/analysis/cycle_model.py`. Part 1 checks the model against simulation and the board; Parts 2 and 3 are **predictions** (model, not measured).

## Part 1 - current design (sequential folded engines, 64x64 tiles)

| Layer | Out side | Cycles/px (PERIOD) | Model cycles/tile | Simulated | Diff | Lane util. | DSP |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | 68 | 27 | 124,848 | 124,872 | +24 | 100% | 18 |
| L2 | 66 | 16 | 69,696 | 69,713 | +17 | 56% | 18 |
| L3 | 66 | 16 | 69,696 | 69,720 | +24 | 100% | 18 |
| L4 | 64 | 144 | 589,824 | 589,844 | +20 | 100% | 14 |

Engines 854,149 + byte-stream I/O 72,052 = 926,201 cycles/tile (simulated); board CYCLES mean 926,220.
Frame: 135 tiles x 926,220 / 100 MHz = 1,250.4 ms core; board 1,311.9 ms -> ARM/DMA overhead 61.5 ms.

### Utilization gap

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| MACs per frame, no halo (2,560 x 960 x 540) | 1.327 G |
| MACs per frame as tiled (halo recomputed) | 1.460 G (+10.0%) |
| Ideal time, 68 DSP x 100 MHz | 0.195 s |
| Ideal time, 220 DSP x 100 MHz | 0.060 s |

| MACs per cycle over the whole frame (incl. ARM/DMA) | 11.1 = 16.4% of 68 DSPs |

Cause: each engine keeps its lanes busy (L1/L3/L4 100 %, L2 56 %), but only one engine runs at a time, so on average about 3/4 of the DSPs are idle.

## Part 2 - prediction: whole-frame line-buffer pipeline, all layers at once

Assumptions: no tiles (no halo recomputation); each layer streams with a fixed cycles-per-pixel; lanes = whole output channels x whole kernel steps; requantizers = ceil(C_out / cycles-per-pixel), 2 DSP each; 32-bit output stream (4 bytes/cycle); fill = (K-1) rows per layer; no stalls; ARM padding 5.9 ms added (as measured today). Real designs lose some of this to stalls and control.

| DSP budget | Lanes L1/L2/L3/L4 | Cycles/px per layer | DSP used | Limit | Frame cycles | 100 MHz | 125 MHz | 150 MHz |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 68 | 12/4/8/36 | 36/36/32/48 | 68 | compute | 25.16 M | 258 ms / 3.9 fps | 207 ms / 4.8 fps | 174 ms / 5.8 fps |
| 100 | 16/6/16/54 | 27/24/16/32 | 100 | compute | 16.77 M | 174 ms / 5.8 fps | 140 ms / 7.1 fps | 118 ms / 8.5 fps |
| 150 | 24/8/16/72 | 18/18/16/24 | 128 | compute | 12.58 M | 132 ms / 7.6 fps | 107 ms / 9.4 fps | 90 ms / 11.1 fps |
| 200 | 36/9/16/108 | 12/16/16/16 | 179 | compute | 8.44 M | 90 ms / 11.1 fps | 73 ms / 13.6 fps | 62 ms / 16.1 fps |

Line buffers: about 18 BRAM36 (K-1 rows per 3x3 layer) instead of the 70 BRAM tiles of today's five tile RAMs (weights extra; wide weight reads for many lanes may need LUTRAM/registers).

## Part 3 - prediction: one shared engine (same 68 DSP), layers time-shared per tile

Same tiles as today (64x64 + 3 px halo, halo recomputed) and the same activation RAMs; the four per-layer engines are replaced by one engine that gets all DSPs for each layer in turn. Two I/O cases: today's byte-wide load/output (72,052 cycles per tile, not overlapped) and fully overlapped I/O (0). ARM/DMA overhead as measured today (61.5 ms per frame) is added. Model, not measured.

| DSP | Requantizers | Lanes L1/L2/L3/L4 | Cycles/px per layer | Engine cycles/tile | I/O | 100 MHz | 150 MHz | BRAM |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 68 | 6 | 54/48/32/54 | 8/3/8/32 | 215,980 | today (72,052) | 450 ms / 2.2 fps | 321 ms / 3.1 fps | ~70 (today's tile RAMs; weights for all layers in one ROM) |
| 68 | 6 | 54/48/32/54 | 8/3/8/32 | 215,980 | overlapped (0) | 353 ms / 2.8 fps | 256 ms / 3.9 fps | ~70 (today's tile RAMs; weights for all layers in one ROM) |

For comparison, today's four engines: 854,149 engine cycles per tile; measured frame 1,311.9 ms.

## Part 4 - prediction: the r0_125 build (deployed network, one more register per engine run, fabric clock 125 MHz)

Same architecture as today plus one register between the activation RAM mux and the MAC DSP (`docs/paper/fmax_plan.md`). The build exists (branch `r0_125`, routed WNS +0.632 ns at 125 MHz, 0 failing endpoints) and is simulated, but it has NOT run on the board: the frame times below are model predictions. Cycles per tile = board mean 926,220 + 4; ARM/DMA overhead held at 61.5 ms (as in Part 3).

| Clock | Cycles per tile | Core time per frame | Frame time (predicted) | fps (predicted) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 MHz | 926,224 | 1,250.4 ms | 1,311.9 ms | 0.762 |
| 125 MHz | 926,224 | 1,000.3 ms | 1,061.8 ms | 0.942 |

Caveats: the 125 MHz build met timing in Vivado (post-route, not on the board); 150 MHz is NOT shown to close timing. Today's whole system has WNS +0.503 ns at 100 MHz, and a 152.38 MHz trial of the unchanged design failed by 1.693 ns (`docs/paper/timing_150mhz.md`; path: activation RAM -> split-RAM mux -> MAC DSP input), so the 150 MHz figures of Part 3 still need a pipelining change. Do not mix the two 3.9 fps figures: Part 2's is the line-buffer pipeline at 100 MHz, Part 3's is the shared engine at 150 MHz with fully overlapped I/O. The 32-bit stream caps the pipeline at 3 cycles per LR pixel (64-bit would halve that); DSP packing of two INT8 multiplies is not modelled.
